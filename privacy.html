Privacy

Script Call keeps production records and project files on your computer. You choose when to export a PDF, create a portable project, or share a file with someone else.

When enabled, update checks contact GitHub's public release service. Downloading an update contacts the release host. Those services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. The update check does not need your production records.

Keep local backups and review exported call sheets for personal contact details before sharing. The demo images on this website contain fictional production information.

This site uses no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or contact forms added by Squid Productions. Hosting providers may process ordinary request information.

Optional Maps coordinate lookup

Editing a shooting-location Maps link or choosing Resolve coordinates may send that Google Maps URL, your IP address and an app identifier to allowlisted Google Maps hosts to follow a bounded short-link/canonical lookup. Explicit place/pin coordinates can be read locally without a request. Cast/crew and production titles are not included. Manual coordinates remain available; no paid geocoder or new API credentials are used. NWS forecast requests retain separate single-request consent.

Native Apple builds

Script Call by Squid Productions. This notice describes the native macOS, iPhone and iPad builds, prepared 28 October 2026.

Production records stay with the production

Production titles, schedules, cast and crew details, email addresses, phone numbers, notes, company images and issued PDFs are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Script Call does not upload this workspace to a Squid Productions cloud service or require an app account. The production decides which contact details to record and how to use or share them.

You choose when to export or share a PDF or portable project. Review personal details before sharing. A destination you choose in Files or a Save dialog may be managed by your own cloud provider. Exported files, shared copies and device backups have their own retention outside the app.

Local retention and recovery

Production records remain in the workspace until you remove that app data using your device’s storage tools. Delete Production and other recoverable-delete actions retain records and issued PDFs for restoration. They do not permanently erase those records. Local workspace backups retain up to ten backup files.

Optional Maps and weather requests

Editing a shooting-location Google Maps link and leaving the field, or choosing Resolve coordinates, may contact allowlisted Google Maps hosts. The request includes the supplied Maps URL, your connection IP address and a static Script Call app identifier. Explicit place or pin coordinates can be read locally without a request. The native client does not attach app account cookies or stored credentials. Manual coordinates remain available.

An online National Weather Service forecast requires permission for that request. It sends production-location coordinates rounded to four decimal places to api.weather.gov. The provider also receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address and a static app identifier. Your production title and cast/crew contacts are not included. Manual weather and offline solar calculations work without an online forecast.

These providers control their request logs and retention. An ephemeral client session does not prevent provider-side logging. NWS describes stored HTTP request logs and possible extended security retention. Google describes retained service and request data. Read the NWS privacy policy and Google privacy policy. Opening Maps in your external browser or Maps app uses that app’s separate privacy settings.

Local diagnostics and voluntary support

The app records sanitized failure categories and source-code frames locally, with at most 40 entries and 32 KB, and uses a fourteen-day recent-history view. Dismiss local alerts clears that recorded history. No automatic diagnostic upload or memory-dump upload is performed.

Report a Bug lets you review a description, reproduction steps, app version/platform and optional diagnostic summary. You can copy or export it, or open a draft in your mail client. Nothing is sent automatically. Review the draft and remove private production information before sending it.

Voluntary support email goes to alexanderhosier@squidproductions.org. Only the owner of that inbox has access. Messages are used for troubleshooting and follow-up about further issues. There is no fixed retention period: messages remain until manual mailbox cleanup, which may be after about a week or a few months. You can request deletion by emailing that address. No guaranteed deletion deadline is stated.

Updates and privacy choices

Native Apple builds receive app updates through TestFlight or the App Store. They do not use the desktop app’s GitHub update-check/download flow. No advertising, tracking or analytics-upload integration is included in the native app.

You can keep Maps and weather offline, decline each online forecast, omit diagnostics from a report and choose not to send support email. For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support correspondence, email the address above. Requests about production-managed contact records and shared call sheets should go to the production that collected them.

Public Script Call privacy page: scriptcall.squidproductions.org/privacy.html. The bundled notice remains readable offline.
