Privacy

Script Call keeps production records and project files on your computer. You choose when to export a PDF, create a portable project, or share a file with someone else.

When enabled, update checks contact GitHub's public release service. Downloading an update contacts the release host. Those services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. The update check does not need your production records.

Keep local backups and review exported call sheets for personal contact details before sharing. The demo images on this website contain fictional production information.

This site uses no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or contact forms added by Squid Productions. Hosting providers may process ordinary request information.

Optional Maps coordinate lookup

Editing a shooting-location Maps link or choosing Resolve coordinates may send that Google Maps URL, your IP address and an app identifier to allowlisted Google Maps hosts to follow a bounded short-link/canonical lookup. Explicit place/pin coordinates can be read locally without a request. Cast/crew and production titles are not included. Manual coordinates remain available; no paid geocoder or new API credentials are used. NWS forecast requests retain separate single-request consent.
