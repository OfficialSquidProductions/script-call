Script Call 0.3.16 — production planning and clearer calls

Developer ID signed and Apple notarized for Apple Silicon · macOS13+. Intel Mac, Windows and Linux distributions remain unvalidated.

Release artifacts, signed update metadata and checksums

More production choices. Clearer days ahead.

Script Call 0.3.16 adds every printed crew department, offline solar times, your own company header image, automatic overridable lunch, two-day Advance and O/C printed personal calls.

Department groups and your company image

Choose Assistant Directors, Continuity and all other call-sheet groups, or keep typing a custom department. Existing assignments and IDs stay intact; aliases share a printed band. In Production Data → Production, choose or replace a PNG/JPEG header image, remove it, or restore SP. Images fit proportionally, travel inside your project and are never uploaded.

Sun times for the actual shooting date

Weather & sun calculates sunrise, sunset, civil dawn and evening civil twilight offline from the shoot date, coordinates and location timezone, including DST. Civil twilight uses the sun’s center 6° below the horizon. Optional manual times stay separate and require review after date/location/timezone changes. Missing coordinates or polar events stay explicitly unavailable. These are astronomical estimates: twelve events across three USNO reference cases had a maximum difference of 127.358 seconds (about 2 minutes 7 seconds); terrain, atmosphere and high latitudes can differ further.

Lunch, two-day Advance and On Call

New days default lunch to crew call +6 hours, including midnight and DST rollover. Editing lunch makes that day manual; Use crew call +6 hours restores automatic mode. Existing lunch values and separate meal schedule rows are preserved. Notes & advance shows the next shooting day and day after next, with dates/units and independent notes; production order and gaps remain intact. Called cast and crew marked O/C on that shoot date print O/C instead of a personal call time. O/C means available, not on set. Stored times remain intact, and normal display returns when availability changes. Issued PDFs never change.

Portable reader10 protects the new embedded-image, lunch, solar, Advance and call-presentation state. Legacy projects/import IDs, manual overrides, both templates, recovery/backups, preferences and exact issued PDFs remain supported. PNG/JPEG input is limited to5MB/24MP and normalized to a bounded embedded PNG. Existing online weather/Maps horizon, opaque-link and manual fallback limitations remain. No new credentials or paid service.

Script Call 0.3.13 — notes, calls, time display and Maps weather

Developer ID signed and Apple notarized for Apple Silicon · macOS 13+. Intel Mac, Windows and Linux remain unvalidated.

0.3.13 release notes & checksums · 0.3.13 source

Scene notes and clear call advice.

Daily scene schedule rows now edit notes instead of start times and durations. Notes can follow production scene notes or be a separate daily override, including blank or multiline notes. New call-sheet output keeps page lengths and order while omitting scene timing filler. Historic timing records and issued PDFs remain preserved.

Personal Call stays visible; preparation, release and day offset are under Advanced. New cast calls default to Shooting Call and new crew calls to General crew call. Existing personal times stay unchanged; a missing base stays unset. A valid cast call no longer inherits production wrap as a personal release. Unknown end-time advice is neutral and separate from red real conflicts, which show allowed hours and still let you save and continue.

Choose 24-hour or AM/PM display.

Settings & help offers military 24-hour and civilian 12-hour display. The preference travels with the production and becomes the default for new productions on this device. Stored times and zones do not change. AM/PM is explicit in editable 12-hour fields; incomplete input keeps the last valid saved time. Issued PDFs keep their original format.

Maps coordinates for shoot-day weather.

Explicit place or coordinate-pin data from a shooting-location Google Maps link can fill latitude and longitude for solar and online NWS weather. Manual coordinates and addresses are protected; replacement is explicit, and stale lookup results are discarded. Camera viewport positions are not treated as venues. Supported short links use bounded HTTPS Google Maps lookups; opaque IDs, viewport-only links, unsupported redirects and offline failures may need manual entry. Real short-link resolution is not yet live validated. Coordinates are suggestions to confirm, not verified venue geocoding. Parking remains independent.

NWS covers supported US locations for about seven days. Forecasts use the selected shoot date and effective timezone; missing/out-of-range, failed and stale forecasts are explicit. No paid API or new credentials.

New daily notes, call-default basis, time preference, coordinate provenance and notes-first issued snapshots require reader8 portable compatibility. Older projects remain readable. Production Desk reimport retains manual fields and stable IDs. Recovery, templates, bug reporting and signed updates remain supported.

Scene titles first. Details when you need them.

Scene cards collapse to their number and title, with Collapse all, Expand all and remembered local open state. Type page fractions directly.

Tab/Enter commits and Escape cancels. Deleted scenes leave draft/advance schedules, page totals and availability checks; other times are not shifted.

Portable projects containing scene recovery metadata require this release or newer (reader7). Older projects remain readable; restoring the last scene removes that extension when appropriate. Production Desk reimport preserves deleted scenes and reports changed-source conflicts. Reliable native updates

Fixed a resource-permission issue found during the actual native upgrade test: a logo could become unreadable after installer ownership changes. Shipped resources now have normal public read permissions before signing. Version 0.3.2 is superseded.

The native 0.3.1 → 0.3.3 update passed all twelve acceptance checks, preserving edits, saved templates, issued PDF bytes, import identities, the demo, images, and backup behavior.

Release validation

Passed: 56 core checks, 19 desktop checks, 12 G-Casper checks, 11 Production Desk import checks against the source build, 11 against the packaged build, and all 12 native update acceptance checks.

Included in this release

  • G-Casper-style front/back layouts and customizable Original Narrative templates.
  • Production Desk import and reviewed reimport, with manual edits and issued days preserved.
  • The Last Light demo: eight scenes, sixteen shots, three shooting days, and stable shared/import identities. The separately labeled legacy demo also remains included.
  • Signed update metadata, SHA-512 archive verification, platform compatibility checks, native app signature checks, and an explicit save-and-backup step before restarting.
