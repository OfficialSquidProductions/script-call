Your first call sheet

  1. Start a production. Start with the blank production, create another production, or open an existing portable project.
  2. Set up the day. Review the shooting date, timezone, unit, general call, locations, parking, and emergency information.
  3. Build the calls. Add the daily schedule, cast calls, crew calls, notes, and advance schedule.
  4. Choose a layout. Open Templates & layout, select G-Casper-style or Original Narrative, then choose paper and output preferences.
  5. Preview and review. Check the printed front and back. Resolve required details before using Review & issue.
  6. Export and back up. Use Export PDF for a readable handoff and Export project for an editable .callsheet archive.

Production Desk import

Export your production from Production Desk, then choose Import Production Desk in Script Call. Review the mapping preview before committing changes. Imported calls, performers, crew, timezone, and emergency details still need review.

On a later import, review the comparison against the prior imported baseline. Manual edits, explicit blanks, saved layouts, and issued days are preserved. Keep your original Production Desk export for its embedded PDF attachments.

Cast and crew availability

In Production Data → Cast or Crew, use Cast availability or Crew availability beside Add. Choose a date/person cell to record all-day availability, unavailable, unknown, or specific windows. Use Ends next day for overnight windows. Save explicitly; Cancel leaves the entry unchanged. Red call-sheet warnings explain known conflicts across the full assigned interval. Unknown availability does not confirm availability or create a hard conflict.

Next shooting-day date

Add Day defaults to the calendar day after the final active day in production sequence, even when an older day is selected. For example, October 22 proposes October 23. Edit the proposed date if needed. Duplicate Day keeps the selected source date. Month/year and leap-day transitions are handled automatically, without changing existing dates.

Remove or restore a production

Choose the production, then open Settings & Help → Delete Production. Review its name and the scope before confirming. Its full contents move to Deleted productions in this app profile. Open that list and choose Restore to recover the original records and issued PDFs, even after reopening. Backup happens before delete/restore. Other productions, preferences and external files stay unchanged; deleting the last active production creates a separate blank production.

Report a bug or crash

Email alexanderhosier@squidproductions.org for Script Call bug and crash reports.

  1. Choose Report a Bug in the app’s sidebar or Help menu. Describe what happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it.
  2. Review the full sanitized report and your own description. Remove private production details or personal information before sharing.
  3. Choose Copy, Export, or open a draft in your existing mail client. Review the draft and send it yourself to alexanderhosier@squidproductions.org.

If a mail draft cannot open or the mail link is too long, copy or export the full report and attach or paste it into an email yourself. Opening a draft does not confirm delivery; nothing is sent automatically.

After a crash or interrupted session, reopening the app can offer a nonblocking notice to review or dismiss the local diagnostics. An interrupted session can also result from a force quit or power loss. No project, script, or media contents, credentials, contacts, personal paths, or memory dumps are collected automatically.

Common questions

Does switching templates erase my information?

The same day data stays in the project when templates change. Hidden sections remain editable. G-Casper-style keeps crew on a separate back, and dense content can continue onto extra pages.

Can I revise an issued sheet?

Issued revisions retain their original PDF bytes. Make a new revision for changed information, review it, then issue deliberately. An import never automatically issues a sheet.

Where should I keep a backup?

Use Create local backup and export a portable project. Keep an independent copy somewhere you control. A PDF is a handoff document; keep an editable project too.

How do updates work?

With automatic checks enabled, the app checks after startup and daily. You can also choose Check for updates. Select Download update, then Save, back up & restart. The app verifies the release, saves pending edits, and creates a local backup before installation. Older update-disabled prototypes need one manual installation of the current release. Failed or offline checks leave your current app usable.

Report a bug on GitHub. Avoid including private production records, contact lists, or unredacted call sheets in a public issue.
