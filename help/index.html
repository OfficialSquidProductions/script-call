Scene notes and clear call advice.

Daily scene schedule rows now edit notes instead of start times and durations. Notes can follow production scene notes or be a separate daily override, including blank or multiline notes. New call-sheet output keeps page lengths and order while omitting scene timing filler. Historic timing records and issued PDFs remain preserved.

Personal Call stays visible; preparation, release and day offset are under Advanced. New cast calls default to Shooting Call and new crew calls to General crew call. Existing personal times stay unchanged; a missing base stays unset. A valid cast call no longer inherits production wrap as a personal release. Unknown end-time advice is neutral and separate from red real conflicts, which show allowed hours and still let you save and continue.

Choose 24-hour or AM/PM display.

Settings & help offers military 24-hour and civilian 12-hour display. The preference travels with the production and becomes the default for new productions on this device. Stored times and zones do not change. AM/PM is explicit in editable 12-hour fields; incomplete input keeps the last valid saved time. Issued PDFs keep their original format.

Maps coordinates for shoot-day weather.

Explicit place or coordinate-pin data from a shooting-location Google Maps link can fill latitude and longitude for solar and online NWS weather. Manual coordinates and addresses are protected; replacement is explicit, and stale lookup results are discarded. Camera viewport positions are not treated as venues. Supported short links use bounded HTTPS Google Maps lookups; opaque IDs, viewport-only links, unsupported redirects and offline failures may need manual entry. Real short-link resolution is not yet live validated. Coordinates are suggestions to confirm, not verified venue geocoding. Parking remains independent.

NWS covers supported US locations for about seven days. Forecasts use the selected shoot date and effective timezone; missing/out-of-range, failed and stale forecasts are explicit. No paid API or new credentials.

Your first call sheet

  1. Start a production. Start with the blank production, create another production, or open an existing portable project.
  2. Set up the day. Review the shooting date, timezone, unit, general call, locations, parking, and emergency information.
  3. Build the calls. Add the daily schedule, cast calls, crew calls, notes, and advance schedule.
  4. Choose a layout. Open Templates & layout, select G-Casper-style or Original Narrative, then choose paper and output preferences.
  5. Preview and review. Check the printed front and back. Resolve required details before using Review & issue.
  6. Export and back up. Use Export PDF for a readable handoff and Export project for an editable .callsheet archive.

Production Desk import

Export your production from Production Desk, then choose Import Production Desk in Script Call. Review the mapping preview before committing changes. Imported calls, performers, crew, timezone, and emergency details still need review.

On a later import, review the comparison against the prior imported baseline. Manual edits, explicit blanks, saved layouts, and issued days are preserved. Keep your original Production Desk export for its embedded PDF attachments.

Cast and crew availability

In Production Data → Cast or Crew, use Cast availability or Crew availability beside Add. Choose a date/person cell to record all-day availability, unavailable, unknown, or specific windows. Restricted windows are yellow and labeled; full day is green, and O/C is distinct. Use Ends next day for overnight windows. Save explicitly; Cancel leaves the entry unchanged. Red call-sheet warnings explain known conflicts across the full assigned interval. Unknown availability does not confirm availability or create a hard conflict.

Scene titles first. Details when you need them.

Scene cards collapse to their number and title, with Collapse all, Expand all and remembered local open state. Delete Scene confirms its name and schedule scope, makes a backup and retains the full record in Deleted scenes. Restore it beside Add scene or in Settings & help. Linked schedule IDs/times, script, shots, cast, location and length stay stored. Personal calls remain independent; review them explicitly. Issued PDFs stay unchanged.

Type page fractions directly.

Length (pages) accepts 2 3/8, 3/8, whole pages and exact decimals. Values remain exact eighths; unsupported/incomplete input is kept visible instead of rounded or saved over your last valid length. Tab/Enter commits and Escape cancels. Deleted scenes leave draft/advance schedules, page totals and availability checks; other times are not shifted.

Remove a record and keep a way back.

Delete Cast, Delete Crew and Delete Location confirm the named record and move it to Deleted records. Restore it beside the list or in Settings & help, including after reopening or sharing a portable project. Original IDs, calls, scene links, availability and issued PDFs remain stored; a backup is made first.

See location titles, expand what you need.

Location cards collapse individually, with Collapse all and Expand all. Their open state is remembered on this device. Location timezone inputs are hidden while saved zones remain unchanged. A deleted location still used by a day or scene must be restored or explicitly replaced before issuing another sheet.

Restricted time is yellow.

Cast and crew time windows now use yellow and explicit Restricted labels, including overnight carry-in. Full day stays green; On Call stays distinct and available, not on set. Red conflict warnings and unknown availability keep their meanings.

Location maps and manual addresses

In Production Data → Locations, use the separate Shooting location and Parking / base camp Maps links. Review suggested link text before choosing Use/Replace. You can always edit the address manually. Short or opaque links require opening Maps and copying/entering the address yourself; no online geocoding service is used. Coordinates are retained under Advanced. Existing template-specific basecamp overrides remain independent.

Crew On Call availability

Select a crew availability cell and choose O/C — On Call (available, not on set). This covers that marked date without changing attendance or call times. Other dates remain unknown until entered. O/C persists with the project; portable copies with O/C need the updated reader. Undo/redo and Cancel remain available.

Crew department suggestions

In Production Data → Crew, type in Department to filter suggestions, choose with Arrow/Enter or the mouse, or type any custom name. Tab keeps normal form navigation. Escape closes suggestions without accepting the highlighted choice; typed text is autosaved as before. Choosing a preset stores its exact spelling. Existing custom and mixed-case names are preserved, and crew grouping is not automatically normalized.

Next shooting-day date

Add Day defaults to the calendar day after the final active day in production sequence, even when an older day is selected. For example, October 22 proposes October 23. Edit the proposed date if needed. Duplicate Day keeps the selected source date. Month/year and leap-day transitions are handled automatically, without changing existing dates.

Remove or restore a production

Choose the production, then open Settings & Help → Delete Production. Review its name and the scope before confirming. Its full contents move to Deleted productions in this app profile. Open that list and choose Restore to recover the original records and issued PDFs, even after reopening. Backup happens before delete/restore. Other productions, preferences and external files stay unchanged; deleting the last active production creates a separate blank production.

Report a bug or crash

Email alexanderhosier@squidproductions.org for Script Call bug and crash reports.

  1. Choose Report a Bug in the app’s sidebar or Help menu. Describe what happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it.
  2. Review the full sanitized report and your own description. Remove private production details or personal information before sharing.
  3. Choose Copy, Export, or open a draft in your existing mail client. Review the draft and send it yourself to alexanderhosier@squidproductions.org.

If a mail draft cannot open or the mail link is too long, copy or export the full report and attach or paste it into an email yourself. Opening a draft does not confirm delivery; nothing is sent automatically.

After a crash or interrupted session, reopening the app can offer a nonblocking notice to review or dismiss the local diagnostics. An interrupted session can also result from a force quit or power loss. No project, script, or media contents, credentials, contacts, personal paths, or memory dumps are collected automatically.

Common questions

Does switching templates erase my information?

The same day data stays in the project when templates change. Hidden sections remain editable. G-Casper-style keeps crew on a separate back, and dense content can continue onto extra pages.

Can I revise an issued sheet?

Issued revisions retain their original PDF bytes. Make a new revision for changed information, review it, then issue deliberately. An import never automatically issues a sheet.

Where should I keep a backup?

Use Create local backup and export a portable project. Keep an independent copy somewhere you control. A PDF is a handoff document; keep an editable project too.

How do updates work?

With automatic checks enabled, the app checks after startup and daily. You can also choose Check for updates. Select Download update, then Save, back up & restart. The app verifies the release, saves pending edits, and creates a local backup before installation. Older update-disabled prototypes need one manual installation of the current release. Failed or offline checks leave your current app usable.

Report a bug on GitHub. Avoid including private production records, contact lists, or unredacted call sheets in a public issue.
