The call before the shoot.

  • Organize production records, shooting weeks, scene notes and cast and crew calls.
  • Record availability, restricted hours and On Call days, with clear, nonblocking advice when a personal call starts outside available hours.
  • Keep shooting, parking, basecamp and hospital details together, with separate addresses, Maps links and instructions.
  • Calculate sun times offline, request supported US forecasts, and choose AM/PM or 24-hour times and Fahrenheit or Celsius.
  • Use your company image, customize layouts, and preview G-Casper-style front/back sheets or Original Narrative output on A4 or US Letter.
  • Plan automatic or custom lunch and the next two shooting days. Export PDFs and preserve issued revisions exactly as shared.
  • Import Production Desk plans, share portable projects, make local backups and restore deleted records.

Projects stay on your device. No account or subscription. Weather lookups are optional; calculated sun times are estimates. Read the quick start for forecast coverage, Maps limitations and getting your first sheet out.
