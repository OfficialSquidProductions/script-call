Script Call 0.3.16 — production planning and clearer call sheets

• Crew Department suggestions now include all call-sheet groups, including Assistant Directors and Continuity. Keep typing custom names; existing assignments and stable IDs are preserved. Aliases share their printed group without rewriting saved names.
• Weather & sun shows offline calculated sunrise, sunset, civil dawn and evening civil twilight for the shoot date, coordinates and location timezone, including DST. Civil twilight is the sun’s center 6° below the horizon. Calculated values are estimates: twelve events across three USNO reference cases had a maximum difference of 127.358 seconds (about 2 minutes 7 seconds). Terrain, atmosphere and high latitudes can differ further. Missing/polar events stay unavailable. Optional manual times are retained for review after date/location/timezone changes.
• Choose, replace or remove a per-production company/header image, or restore SP. PNG/JPEG images fit proportionally without cropping or stretching, stay embedded in portable projects, and are never uploaded. Legacy default SP behavior remains until explicitly changed; removal persists. Up to 5 MB / 24 megapixels input, normalized to a bounded embedded PNG.
• New shooting days default lunch to six hours after crew call, including midnight and DST rollover. A manual lunch edit preserves its date offset and stops following later crew-call edits. Use crew call + 6 hours restores automatic mode. Legacy lunch values and independent schedule meal rows remain intact.
• Notes & advance and new PDFs show the next shooting day and day after next separately, following production order with clear dates and units. Both slots support independent manual notes; existing Advance text is preserved. Missing days/schedules stay explicit, and long notes continue safely across pages.
• Called cast and crew marked O/C on that shoot date print O/C instead of their personal call time. O/C means available, not on set. Stored times remain intact and normal display returns when availability changes. Already issued PDFs are unchanged.

Preserves project/import/person/day IDs, custom departments, manual overrides, availability, production and record recovery, G-Casper/Original Narrative and other layouts, local reporting, backups, privacy preferences and signed updates. New fields and snapshots use portable reader 10; older projects remain readable. Test-only demo data is excluded from app resources.

Mac download: Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later. Intel Mac, Windows and Linux distributions are not validated. Solar calculations work offline; online NWS weather still needs supported US coordinates, connectivity and forecast horizon. Opaque/viewport-only Maps links may require manual coordinates/address review; live short-link behavior remains limited. No paid API or new credentials are introduced.
