Script Call 0.3.11

• Delete individual cast, crew and locations with named confirmation and recovery from Deleted records or Settings & help. Original IDs, calls, scene links and availability remain stored; a backup is made first. Restore reactivates retained links without overwriting other edits. Nothing is permanently erased.
• Location cards collapse to their title, with Collapse all/Expand all and remembered local expansion. Delete Location is at the bottom of the expanded card.
• Restricted cast/crew availability windows are yellow and explicitly labeled, including overnight carry-in. Full day stays green; O/C stays distinct and means available, not on set. Conflict warnings remain explicit.
• Location timezone controls are hidden while saved zones and daily time semantics are preserved. A deleted linked location must be restored or explicitly replaced before another sheet is issued; another location is never selected automatically.

Original issued PDFs remain unchanged. Portable projects with deleted member/location records need this release or newer. Old saved projects and Production Desk import remain supported. Previous Maps-link/manual address and O/C features remain intact; short or opaque Maps links still require manual address entry.

Apple Silicon macOS 13 or newer. Windows, Intel Mac and Linux distribution are not validated. Existing projects and historical releases are preserved.
