Script Call 0.3.12

• Scene cards collapse to their scene number and title, with Collapse all/Expand all and remembered local open state. New scenes open for editing.
• Delete Scene confirms the selected scene and linked schedule scope, makes a backup and retains the full record for Restore. Recover from Deleted scenes beside Add scene or in Settings & help. Original IDs, scripts, shots, cast, locations, page lengths and schedule links/times stay stored. Independent personal calls remain unchanged and should be reviewed explicitly. Nothing is permanently erased.
• Length (pages) accepts mixed fractions such as 2 3/8, fraction-only values such as 3/8, whole pages and exact decimals. Values stay at exact eighth-page precision; unsupported/incomplete input is kept visible for correction without rounding or overwriting the saved length. Tab/Enter commits; Escape cancels the edit. Labels clarify page units instead of raw eighth counts.
• Deleted scenes leave draft/advance schedules, page totals and availability checks. Schedule movement skips retained hidden links. Restore reactivates retained current relationships without overwriting other edits. Original issued snapshots and PDF bytes remain unchanged.

Projects with scene-recovery metadata require this release or newer when sharing a portable project (reader7). Existing saved projects, Production Desk import, templates, Maps/manual-address fallback, O/C availability, member/location/production recovery, reporting and signed updates remain supported. Short or opaque Maps links still require manual address entry.

Apple Silicon macOS13+. Windows, Intel Mac and Linux distribution remain unvalidated. No App Store submission.
